What are my commissions like?

I mean, you can see my work below, all personal for now because I haven't had any commissions yet - which means that if you were to ask for a commission, you would be the world's first! I generally go for a more geometric style but that mainly comes from my method of modelling, which is using Fusion 360 to make a model and putting the model into Blender to colour it and animate it. Only reason that I do this is because I know Fusion really well, but am still learning Blender. To be honest, I am not the best at the Blender side of things, but it don't matter for basic stuff you know. I also slightly prefer Fusion to Blender because of how specific I can be about measurements. In fact, I've even taught a few of my friends a little bit of Fusion before which is coolio.

How do I get a commission?

To get a commission, I would need money, but for now they are COMPLETELY FREE! That's right, literally just contact me and I will at least attempt to 3D model whatever you want - though my timescale is very... fragile right now so as of 17/09/26, don't expect anything to be made quickly. Anyway, if you want to know more, check the "I am poor" page! Meanwhile, enjoy my work!
